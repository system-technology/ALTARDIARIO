altarDiario (hereinafter, "the App", "we", or "the controller")

This policy describes what personal data we collect, why we use it, with whom it is shared, and what your rights are.

2. What Data We Collect and Why

We only process data strictly necessary for the App to function. We do not sell data to third parties or build advertising profiles.

2.3 Observability and Security

  • Crashlytics (Firebase): Crash reports with user identifier (UID only) — no personal data.
  • Analytics (Firebase): Product events with sanitized parameters: only numbers, booleans, and closed-catalog values (e.g., "reading", "reflection", "prayer"). Never free text, emails, tokens, or user content.
  • App Check (Firebase): Device attestation (Play Integrity / DeviceCheck / reCAPTCHA v3) to protect APIs from abuse.

2.4 Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). The FCM token is stored in users/{uid}/private/account (private) and is used exclusively to send daily reading reminders and social activity notifications (new followers, comments, reactions, "amens", debate replies, mentions). You can disable them in App Settings or system permissions.

2.5 Local Storage

  • SharedPreferences: Local UID for offline session, notification preferences (hour/minute), downloaded reading plan.
  • SQLite (sqflite): Downloaded Bibles for offline reading.

3. What Is Public vs. Private

Public (anyone can read): profile (name, photo, bio, stats), reflections, comments, prayer requests, debates, replies, reactions, votes, following/followers.
Private (only you or participants): email address, FCM token, 1:1 chats, notifications, highlights, Bible notes, general notes, blocked list, auth credentials.

Firestore security rules (audited and tested) guarantee that no one can read or write another user's private data. Social counters (likes, votes, "amens") only change by ±1 and only by adding/removing your own UID.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Contract performance / user request: authentication, saving your content, cross-device sync, notifications you asked for.
  • Consent: analytics (optional, disableable), push notifications (optional, system permission).
  • Legitimate interest: security (App Check), stability (Crashlytics), abuse/spam prevention.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Account and content data: kept while the account exists.
  • Account deletion: from Profile → "Delete Account". This deletes your public profile and the private private/account document in a single atomic operation. Once task T-076 is integrated, your published content (reflections, comments, prayers, debates, replies, chats, notes, highlights) will also be deleted.
  • You can also request deletion by email to privacidad@altardiario.app (we respond within ≤ 30 days).
  • Crashlytics logs: 90 days (Firebase policy).
  • Analytics events: 14 months (Firebase policy).

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

You may exercise at any time:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object

Write to privacidad@altardiario.app with subject "GDPR Rights" and your UID or account email.

7. International Transfers

Firebase (Google) hosts data in EU and US data centers. Google provides Standard Contractual Clauses and EU-US / Swiss-US Privacy Framework certifications to ensure an equivalent level of protection.

8. Minimum Age

The App is intended for users aged 13+ (or the digital consent age in your country, whichever is higher). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we detect an underage account, we delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will post the updated version at this same URL with the revision date. Material changes will be notified in the App (notification or banner) with reasonable notice.
